Academic Projects

Many of my current projects are connected to coursework in communication and digital media. These projects often focus on understanding how people interact with technology and how information is presented online.

This website itself is an example of an academic project, designed to practice writing structured, semantic HTML while following accessibility and usability principles.

Through these assignments, I am learning how thoughtful structure improves clarity and helps users navigate content more easily.

Future Goals

Moving forward, I would like to continue building skills in web development, particularly in accessible design and user-focused content creation.

I am interested in exploring how design decisions impact different users, especially in educational and informational contexts.

Long term, my goal is to apply these skills in professional settings where clear communication and ethical technology use are valued.
