Academic Projects

Many of my current projects are connected to coursework in communication and digital media. These projects often focus on understanding how people interact with technology and how information is presented online. At my Internship we have already been working on a lot of projects that are realatingh back to things we have learned over the course of being at the university and it is funseeing it all come together in a real world setting.

This website itself is an example of an academic project, designed to practice writing structured, semantic HTML while following accessibility and usability principles. I have added to it over time to include new sections and content as I learn more about web development. Over the courtse of the semester, I have been able to apply concepts from class to enhance the design and functionality of this site.

Through these assignments, I am learning how thoughtful structure improves clarity and helps users navigate content more easily. I am also gaining experience in using HTML elements appropriately to convey meaning and create a better user experience. These skills will be valuable as I continue to explore web development and create more complex projects in the future.

Future Goals

Moving forward, I would like to continue building skills in web development, particularly in accessible design and user-focused content creation. I am interested in exploring how different design choices can impact user experience and accessibility, especially for diverse audiences with varying needs.

I am interested in exploring how design decisions impact different users, especially in educational and informational contexts. I want to learn how to create content that is not only visually appealing but also easy to understand and navigate for a wide range of users, including those with disabilities.

Long term, my goal is to apply these skills in professional settings where clear communication and ethical technology use are valued. I hope to work on projects that have a positive impact and contribute to making information more accessible and engaging for everyone.
